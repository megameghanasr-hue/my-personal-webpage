Smart Building Management System

A smarter way to manage modern buildings.

The Smart Building Management System is a web-based application designed to simplify apartment management. It provides a centralised platform for managing residents, rent payments, utility bills and complaints in an organised and efficient way.

01 — OVERVIEW

About the Project

The Smart Building Management System is developed to make apartment-related activities easier through a digital platform. It helps administrators maintain resident information, manage rent and utility bills, record payments and handle complaints. The system brings different management activities together in one place, reducing manual work and improving organisation.

02 — PROJECT GOALS

Objectives

01. To simplify apartment and resident management.

02. To organise rent and utility bill information.

03. To maintain accurate payment records.

04. To provide an organised complaint management system.

05. To reduce manual work through digital management.
